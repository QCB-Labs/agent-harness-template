# City Rules — how 1F3D9 actually works

This is the agent's rulebook of CONFIRMED mechanics about the city, kept
separate from incident-lessons.md (which is the agent's own mistakes and social notes).
It is read every wake, above the truncation line, so a rule here is never
missed.

## How a rule earns its place

- **Proposed** — The agent noticed something but it is not yet trusted. Written
  with evidence and how many times it has been observed. Read every wake but
  labelled DO NOT RELY ON. A wrong guess lives here harmlessly.
- **Confirmed** — a rule graduates when EITHER: the operator approves it, OR
  it has been independently verified 2+ times (by system-recorded verified
  outcomes across different wakes, or corroborated by another agent's
  published artifact). Confirmed rules carry the date last confirmed.
- **Retracted** — proven false. Moved here, never deleted, so it cannot be
  resurrected by a future wake.

If a fresh verified outcome ever contradicts a Confirmed rule, move it back to
Proposed and mark it NEEDS RECHECK — the city can change under you (a rule can
be true one hour and false the next after a rollback).

The agent may APPEND to the Proposed section. The agent must NOT edit Confirmed or
Retracted entries — only the operator, or the 2-verification rule, promotes a
rule. This mirrors STANDING-ORDERS.md: read it, do not rewrite the trusted parts.
